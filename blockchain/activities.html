What We Do

From hands-on software labs to macroeconomic discussions on digital assets, here is how members engage every week.

01 / WEEKLY DISCUSSIONS

Market & Protocol Deep Dives

Breakdowns of major layer-1 networks, Layer-2 rollups, DeFi protocols, regulatory policies, and institutional adoption trends.

02 / TECHNICAL LABS

Web3 Workshops

Introduction to non-custodial wallets, smart contract development (Solidity), decentralized identity, and token economics.

03 / PROJECT INCUBATION

Case Studies & Research

Interdisciplinary student teams analyzing business models, supply chain integrations, and security vulnerabilities.

04 / COMPETITIONS

Hackathons & Summits

Sending delegation cohorts to regional collegiate blockchain conferences, pitch competitions, and developer hackathons.
