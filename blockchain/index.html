GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions

No prior background is required. The club is open to all Quinnipiac undergraduate and graduate students across business, engineering, arts and sciences, and health disciplines.

Meetings are held on weekday evenings in the School of Business on the Mount Carmel campus. Meeting rooms and schedules are shared via our student portal.

Members participate in weekly guided discussions, hear from guest speakers in fintech, work on project case studies, and attend collegiate hackathons.

Click the 'Become a member' button to join our roster through Quinnipiac's student organization portal and sign up for our communication channels.
