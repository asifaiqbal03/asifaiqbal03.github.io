About the Club

Quinnipiac Blockchain is an undergraduate and graduate student organization situated in the School of Business at Quinnipiac University. Our mission is to demystify distributed ledger technology and bring actionable crypto education to campus.

Our Mission & Purpose

We bridge academic theory and real-world fintech applications. Through collaborative workshops, weekly debates, and networking events, our members develop a deep literacy in decentralized technologies, tokenomics, and smart contract design.
