Our Principles

Core Values

The standards that guide our community, research, and campus presence.

1. Education Over Speculation

We focus on technical merit, cryptography, system architecture, and economic models rather than hype, short-term price movements, or trading advice.

2. Cross-Disciplinary Inclusion

Blockchain is not just for computer science or finance. We actively encourage students from health sciences, legal studies, communications, and liberal arts to share their perspectives.

3. Critical Thinking & Ethics

We objectively evaluate the trade-offs of decentralized systems, including regulatory considerations, governance challenges, security pitfalls, and environmental impacts.
